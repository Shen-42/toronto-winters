Were Toronto Winters as Snowy as I Remember as a Kid?

Adapted from Were Kyiv Winters as Snowy as I Remember as a Kid? by Volodymyr Agafonkin. Data: Environment and Climate Change Canada, stations TORONTO CITY (Climate ID 6158355) and TORONTO NORTH YORK (615S001).

Snow on the ground, day by day

Each row is one winter, November to April. Darker means deeper snow. Shaded rows are my childhood winters, 2006–07 to 2015–16.

Days with snow on the ground

Days with at least 1 cm, November to April. Dark bars are my childhood winters.

Most winters come from the downtown station (TORONTO CITY), about 5 km from Bayview and Eglinton. It has no snow depth readings for 2009–10, 2010–11, 2011–12 and 2014–15. Winters marked * are filled from the North York station, about 11 km away. In the two winters both stations recorded, North York had 17 more days of snow cover in 2012–13 and 4 fewer in 2013–14, so the filled winters are close but not exact. Blank days inside other winters are treated as no snow.
